Cross-host validation conclusion
================================

The direction-masked detector was evaluated across 12 ordered host-held-out
folds using the NF-UNSW-NB15 NetFlow v3 dataset.

Mean AUROC was 0.9957 with a standard deviation of 0.0064. Mean average
precision was 0.9970 with a standard deviation of 0.0051. Mean recall was
0.9988 with a standard deviation of 0.0026, indicating strong ranking and
attack-retrieval performance across unseen source hosts.

Precision averaged 0.8662, while MCC averaged 0.5640 with a standard
deviation of 0.3024. The larger variability in MCC indicates that the
operating point is less stable than the ranking metrics.

The calibrated threshold varied from 0.00008 to 0.99383 across folds. This
large variation indicates score-distribution shift between source hosts.
Therefore, a single globally fixed threshold cannot yet be claimed to
maintain the requested false-positive target on unseen hosts.

The result supports the feasibility of direction-preserving threat ranking,
but not deployment-ready threshold transfer. Further evaluation must report
benign false alerts, false alerts per hour, and threshold-transfer behavior
under realistic benign-dominant traffic prevalence.
